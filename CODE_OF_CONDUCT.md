# Code of Conduct

The Forever Project is a small, friendly fan project. We want everyone who reports a bug, fixes a route or
asks a question to feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here range from first-time contributors to experienced developers, and from
  new players to speedrunners.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Explain what is wrong and, where you can, how to fix it.
- Respect the maintainer's decisions about scope and direction, and other people's time.
- Credit other people's work, and respect the source rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including account or character details they have not shared.
- Sexual content or violent imagery.
- Spam, advertising, and posting cheats, exploits or account-selling services.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, close issues or pull requests, and block
people from the repository when this code is not followed. Depending on how serious it is, the response may
be a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or open an issue
asking the maintainer (@liviu-stefan-pit) to get in touch if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are
handled as privately as possible.
