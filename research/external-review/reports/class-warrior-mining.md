# Horde Warrior mining

Orc, Troll, Tauren, Undead, and Horde Skyborne keep Mining and Blacksmithing for dps and tank. This pass does not change that pair. It does not add a mining circuit, and it does not add Ragefire weapon quests or Veteran Uzzek.

One tip, every race and both roles: Mine ore nodes that are already on the route. Do not add a mining circuit. Starter copper veins were cut in the starting zones. If bars are short, buy them or skip that craft.

`research/professions/class_professions.json` is the target. `verified` stays false.
