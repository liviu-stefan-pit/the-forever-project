## What the notes support

- Hearth: inns still grant rested XP (Mythic Store, 2026-09-21). The Crossroads step is the first inn of that hub. Innkeeper Toka in Riverglades is the later hub inn, not a capital.
- Dungeons: Mythic Store says the first quested clear is the valuable run and repeat kill XP is very little. Warcraft Tavern (2026-11-20) says each dungeon can be entered at most five times an hour. Kill XP inside was raised 50% on 2026-11-09, then zeroed for the party when any member is more than 12 levels above the enemy (Kaivax, 2026-11-15). That is why the tip treats inside kills as not a grind.
- Camp: Blizzard’s camping article says a Basic Campfire is Cooking 1 and is the shared cooldown object. Rested XP is the Leatherworking Camp Tent at skill 20: 5% of a level, and nothing if rested is already above that. The tip does not require Leatherworking.
- Legacy: Icy Veins. Talented’s five ranks move the first talent point to 9/8/7/6/5 and need 5 Adventure points plus 5 in Well Rested. Launch spend cap is 16 per character. A first character has few points, so this is an alt note on the level-1 accept.
- Layer: the 2026-11-20 addon notes obfuscate realm ids so addons stop targeting one layer. Switching without picking a layer is not described as removed. The tip only says not to change layer during Plainstrider Menace.
- Riding: games.gg and Hardcore Helper (updated 2026-11-16) say Apprentice riding at level 40 costs 100 gold and includes the first racial mount at 60% speed. Mythic Store (2026-09-21) says the level and gold were not published. The November hotfix log does not state the training level or the base gold. `whyShort` marks this unverified.
