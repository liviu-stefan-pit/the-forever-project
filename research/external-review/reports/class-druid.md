WoW Forever DB headlines (rated 11 Nov 2026):

- Feral Cat: Alchemy S, Herbalism B. Best pair named as Alchemy and Herbalism.
- Balance: Alchemy S, Herbalism A. Same pair.
- Restoration: Alchemy S, Herbalism S. Same pair.

Rejected pairs (same two on every row):

1. Leatherworking + Skinning. Leatherworking is the next crafting primary (A on Feral Cat and Restoration, B on Balance). Skinning does not feed Alchemy.
2. Engineering + Mining. Engineering is A on Balance and Restoration and C on Feral Cat. Mining is D or F on these lists and does not supply herbs.

Secondaries on every row: First Aid, Cooking, Fishing. They do not take a primary slot. First Aid is S on Feral Cat and Balance and A on Restoration. Cooking is B. Fishing is F.
