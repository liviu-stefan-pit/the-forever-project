# Horde Hunter professions

Role is dps only. Beast Mastery notes say the pet tanks while the hunter stays at range. No Forever note says a pet-tank or melee hunter should take a different profession pair, so Survival and Marksmanship stay on this same pair.

Legal races: Orc, Troll, Tauren, Horde Skyborne (Windshaper). Public race pages list Hunter on the Horde Skyborne class list. Undead is skipped. Tame quests 6062, 6083, and 6082 use race mask 130 (Orc + Troll). Quest 6081’s mask is 146, which includes the Undead bit, but that quest only follows the Orc/Troll chain and no Forever note says Forsaken can be Hunters.

Every included race uses the same primary pair: Skinning and Leatherworking. Hunters kill beasts, those corpses are the leather, and the armor is leather then mail. Secondaries for every race: First Aid, Cooking, and Fishing. They do not take a primary slot. Ammo is UNKNOWN: the hunter web note never says Forever still uses arrows or bullets, so there is no ammo stop.

Rejected for every race, not a full pair list:

- Mining + Engineering. Ore is a node circuit beside the beast quests. Starter copper veins were cut. Engineering gadgets are not required to level the class.
- Herbalism + Alchemy. Herbs are a second circuit. Agility potions are consumables, not the armor the class wears.

Do not treat Triage (6622), Clamlette Surprise (6610), or Nat Pagle, Angler Extreme (6607) as required. Each is its own trip after the starter zones. Rank-up books bought from a vendor you already visit can stay; those three quests should not.

## Orc (dps)

Pair: Skinning + Leatherworking.

Skinning: Kagil, Valley of Trials, Durotar, about 40.8, 67.8, Apprentice only (npc 267327). Leatherworking: Kamari, Orgrimmar, The Drag, about 63.0, 45.0 (npc 5811). Quest 6081 already sends the hunter to Ormak Grimshot in Orgrimmar (npc 3352, about 66.05, 18.53) after the Durotar tames, so the first leatherworking lesson is that city visit, not a new farm.

Tame, still inside Durotar, from Thotar at Razor Hill (npc 3171, about 51.85, 43.49): 6062 Dire Mottled Boar, then 6083 Surf Crawler, then 6082 Armored Scorpid. 6081 is only “speak to Ormak.” Breadcrumbs 6068, 6069, and 6070 are alternate ways to reach Thotar. Do not add them as a second loop. City breadcrumbs from Thunder Bluff or Orgrimmar are not the Orc start.

Class trainer checkpoint: Thotar while the tame chain is active, then Ormak for later ranks. No separate ammo checkpoint.

## Troll (dps)

Same pair, same Durotar and Orgrimmar trainers, same quests 6062, 6083, 6082, and 6081. Troll shares race mask 130 with Orc. Nothing in Questie gives Trolls a different hunter chain.

## Tauren (dps)

Pair: Skinning + Leatherworking.

Skinning: Nawka Wildsong, Red Cloud Mesa, about 44.6, 76.8, Apprentice only (npc 267330). Later ranks: Yonn Deepcut, Bloodhoof Village, about 44.97, 61.4 (npc 6290). Leatherworking: Chaw Stronghide, Bloodhoof, about 45.0, 61.4 (npc 3069), the same camp as the hunter trainer Yaw Sharpmane (npc 3065, about 46.93, 59.65).

Tame, still in Mulgore, from Yaw: 6061 Adult Plainstrider, then 6087 Prairie Stalker, then 6088 Swoop. 6089 is only “speak to Holt Thunderhorn” on Hunter Rise (npc 3039, about 57.3, 89.79). Breadcrumbs 6065, 6066, and 6067 only point at Yaw. 96130 Chakuyak is a pelt quest from Yaw after 757. It is not a profession grind and not part of the tame chain. Do not add it as a required farm.

## Horde Skyborne (dps)

Pair: Skinning + Leatherworking. Windshaper class list includes Hunter.

Leatherworking: Indari Sunseam, Zephras Isle, about 44.6, 44.4, Apprentice (npc 251993). That spot is next to Elayaa Easewind, the hunter trainer who starts 94007 (npc 254084, about 45.26, 44.24). 94007 is “report to Quel'ana Quickgale in Valanaar” (npc 252389, about 59.56, 72.63). Race mask is both Skyborne bits. No `next` quest is stored on 94007. Quel'ana’s npc record also names quests 94013 and 94050, and those ids are absent from `quests.json`, so any later tame steps are UNKNOWN and are not a required farm.

Skinning trainers on Zephras in the trainer file are Ishlee Breezewhisper (Valanaar, about 59.6, 76.0, npc 257003) and Mendalass Tattermend (Shen'dar Village, about 43.2, 43.4, npc 257024). Both rows are tagged Artisan with a level-35 gate. Whether either also teaches Apprentice is UNKNOWN. Do not send a Skyborne hunter on a mining loop while that rank is unknown. Mining trainers on the island have unknown skill caps.

## Skipped

Undead. No Forever source in the web notes says Forsaken can be Hunters. The only Undead bit on a hunter quest here is the follow-up mask on 6081, which still starts from the Orc/Troll chain.
