# Horde Druid feral bear tank addendum

Verified: false. This adds the tank role the accepted Druid plan skipped. Races stay Tauren and Horde Skyborne. Orc, Undead, and Troll stay out.

## Pair

The pair stays Herbalism + Alchemy. The Feral Bear list (rated 11 Nov 2026) names that as the best pair: Alchemy S, Herbalism B. Tank can use Alchemy. Flasks and Free Action work in form, and skill-ups still cost herbs. No different pair.

| Race | Role | Pair |
|---|---|---|
| Tauren | tank | Herbalism + Alchemy |
| Skyborne | tank | Herbalism + Alchemy |

Rejected pairs, same as the other Druid rows:

1. Leatherworking + Skinning. Leatherworking is the next crafter (A). Skinning does not feed Alchemy.
2. Engineering + Mining. Engineering is C. Bombs cannot be thrown in Bear Form. Mining is D.

Secondaries stay First Aid, Cooking, and Fishing. First Aid is S on the bear list. Cooking is B. Fishing is F. They do not take a primary slot.

## Tip

One rule on every Druid row, both races, feral dps, balance, restoration, and tank: pick herbs that are already on the route being walked. Do not add a node circuit or a detour to fill Alchemy. If a herb is not on the way, skip it. The bear list’s own Herbalism note is the same idea: herbs come from travel between quests, in form, at no shift cost.
