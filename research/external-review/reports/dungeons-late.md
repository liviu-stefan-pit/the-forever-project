# Late Horde dungeons — one quested clear each

Inside kill credit is the clear. Do not reset for another pass, and do not add a farm loop. `pre.single` is any one prerequisite. `pre.group` is all of them. Horde rows use races `8589934770`. Races `0` is neutral. Coordinates below are Questie spawn percents. Instance corpses at `-1, -1` have no pin.

Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace are out of scope. Ragefire, Wailing Caverns, the Ruins, and the mid dungeons are out of scope.

## Blackrock Depths (51-60)

One clear: the Kargath kill quests, the Thunderbrew recipe, the heart, Ribbly, the princess rescue, and the dark iron legacy. OpenXP's 51-60 file is that set (4134, 7201, 3907, 4081, 4082, 4132, 4122, 4063, 4123, 4136, 4024, 4324, 4002, 4133).

Speedrun never turns it in, so the guard drops **3443** and everything that needs the torch: **3452**, **3453**, **3454**, **3462**, **3463**, **3481**, **4022**. **4023** and **4024** both want the Black Dragonflight Molt from that torch. ## Lower and Upper Blackrock Spire (54-60)

One Lower clear and one Upper clear. Lower is the seal pieces, the eggs, Bijou, Warlord's Command, and the tablet. Upper is **4743** (the seal made on the Lower clear), **4974**, **7761** if the command letter drops, and the General. The Rexxar skull tests and the blue-flight catalogue stay as the quests that send you there once. Do not reset Lower for extra eggs. OpenXP stops at Lower. WoW-Pro Forever has a separate Upper file. Both are one lockout each, not a farm.

Endgame chains with a missing prerequisite stay out: **8921** and the Bodley line need quests that are not in this quest file or not turned in earlier (**8922**, **8960**). They are not in the step pack. Do not add them.
