# What WoW Forever adds beyond Classic (research notes, Orion)

WoW: Forever is Blizzard's permanent "reimagined original world" game mode. It launched globally on
**4 November 2026**. The level cap stays 60. It sits next to modern WoW and Classic, and it is set after
Warcraft III: Reforged and before Molten Core. All text here is our own summary. Every point names its source.
**Verified** means we read it on a Blizzard page or on Wowhead's copy of a Blizzard post. **Unverified** means it
comes from one third-party site only.

## XP modifiers (1-20)

XP to 20 is the Classic total, 167,200. The route assumes no heirlooms, XP potions, battle pass, guild XP, or recruit-a-friend XP.

The default guide sim is the worst case: no rested, no food, dungeons off.

Profile "XP bonuses you have":

- Well Fed: +5% kill XP.
- Rested policy: none, from the game (`Profile.xpRested`), or one inn rest (5% of a level).
- Legacy Well Rested: +4% rested rate and cap per rank. The sim uses 0 unless the account already has Legacy points.
- Cozy Sleeping Bag: +3% all XP from level 14 only. The route tells you to skip that chain until after 20.

These are not XP bonuses. Warchief's Blessing is health and haste and drops on death. Fish Bowl is +8% stats. A Camp Tent fills rested only up to 5% of a level and has no effect above that. That cap is intended.

When dungeons are on, dungeon kill XP in that walk is 60% of the Classic mob table, once. Dungeon quest XP is the table value and is not doubled again. Two dungeon quests, 95216 and 92422, have no XP in Questie, so the sim does not invent one.

Green mobs give less kill XP than Classic. The sim already uses that curve.

The client recompute applies bonuses to the open guide only, using the baked step split. A buff earned in an earlier pack does not move the next pack's expected level.

Sources: [Blizzard hotfixes, 20 November 2026](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-20-2026), [Wowhead: dungeon XP is 60% of Classic Era](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/dungeon-experience-in-wow-forever-is-now-60-of-classic-era-blizzard-happy-with-383831).

## WoW Forever content inside 40-60 (Horde)

- **Riverglades (36-44).** Horde hub Rog'mar, flight master Grakna at 59.6, 45.0 (flights to Stonard, Kargath, Flame Crest, Hammerfall).
  Neutral Powderfuse Port has a boat to Steamwheedle Port in Tanaris. Before you go, bring 10 Chunk of Boar Meat, 10 Heavy Weightstone,
  12 Iron Bar and 4 Thick Leather for Rog'mar quests. Breadcrumbs: Report to Rog'mar (Grom'gol), Finding Tan'jani (Hammerfall).
  Sources: [Wowhead zone overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/riverglades-zone-overview), [Wowhead Riverglades news](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/news/riverglades-is-one-of-the-best-leveling-zones-in-wow-forever-383849), [foreverquestlog](https://foreverquestlog.com/zones/riverglades/). Unverified beyond those.
- **Krol'dok Stronghold (40-45).** Six bosses, entrance cave at 37.5, 37.5. Horde quests come from Rog'mar (Eye of Mugg from Krom'rosh; Key Ingredients
  from Kreza Darkthorn, which takes 4 Thick Leather and 12 Iron Bar). Source: [warcraftmaps](https://warcraftmaps.com/dungeons/kroldok-stronghold). Unverified.
- **Alcaz Prison (48-53), Blackmaw Hold (55-60), Shaper's Terrace (58-60; uses Un'Goro power crystals at its consoles).** Verified (Blizzard blog via Wowhead).
- **Mount Hyjal (55-60, quests need level 54).** Reached through Darkwhisper Gorge in Winterspring. Horde camp: Zet'gan at about 13.8-14.0, 54.8 and Kal'tinzan at 13.8, 53.1.
  "To the Summit of Eternity" is reported as Horde quest id 95248. Third-party counts give about 90 quests open to a Horde character, worth roughly the XP from 58 to 60,
  so our **speedrun** finishes 58-60 in Hyjal and **balanced** offers Silithus as well. Sources: [wowforeveraddon](https://www.wowforeveraddon.com/guides/quests/mount-hyjal/),
  [wofwforever](https://wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mount-hyjal-access/), [khorium](https://khorium.pro/guide-view.html?slug=wow-forever-mount-hyjal-quests), [bytetavern](https://bytetavern.com/wow-forever-mount-hyjal/). All unverified.
- **Shen'dralas (quests 38-49).** South of Desolace through the Valley of Bones, no flight master in the zone; Horde rides in from Shadowprey Village.
  Its opening quest starts with Uthek the Wise in Desolace (needs the Classic quest Ongeku). Sources: PR #5 data, [bytetavern](https://bytetavern.com/wow-forever-shendralas/), [polestar](https://polestar.guide/guides/40-50/shendralas). Unverified.
- **Mount Hyjal lead-ins (PR #5 data).** Several Hyjal chains need Forever quests handed out elsewhere: Moonglade (The Ancient, Orokai; Stolen Power; The Pact, from Dendrite Starblaze),
  Winterspring (Crate Expectations, from Nami in Everlook) and Thousand Needles (Praise). Unverified.
- **Zephras Isle** is a level 1-13 Skyborne start zone; its only level-40 quests are riding quests for that race, so it has no steps in 40-60.
- **UiMap ids** for new zones (from the QuestieDB Forever DBC notes): Riverglades 2548, Mount Hyjal 2482, Shen'dralas 2652, Zephras Isle 2521, Darkspear Islands 2524.
  Source: [QuestieDB docs/forever-data.md](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/docs/forever-data.md).

## Limits of the data we used

**Update (PR #5):** QuestieDB's release build `v1.1.3` (`QuestieDB-1.1.3-Forever.zip`, imported by Godfather into `content/questie-db/`) **does** contain
WoW Forever-new content: 1112 Horde/neutral quests, 2328 NPCs, 157 objects and 645 items flagged `forever: true`. The route now takes its Forever quest ids,
givers, turn-ins and coordinates from there (Riverglades 67 quests, Tanaris 12, Shen'dralas 23, Azshara 25, Mount Hyjal 129 including lead-ins). They stay
`verified: false` because the flag means only "absent from the Era build", not "checked in game". Sources: [QuestieDB v1.1.3](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/releases/tag/v1.1.3), PR #5 in this repo.
The NPC coordinates we had from fan sites (Grakna 59.6, 45.1; Krom'rosh 59.4, 45.6; Zet'gan 13.8, 54.8; Kal'tinzan 13.8, 53.1) match PR #5.

The older note below is about QuestieDB's `data/Forever` folder on master, which we used for the Classic quests:

QuestieDB `data/Forever` is a **converted Classic Era baseline**: Classic ids, with zone coordinates moved to Forever map bounds (only Mulgore,
Eastern Plaguelands, Redridge and Stormwind maps changed). It does **not** contain the new Forever quests, NPCs or dungeons
([conversion.json](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/data/Forever/conversion.json), [forever-data.md](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/master/docs/forever-data.md)).
So every Forever-new step in our files is `verified: false` and has `tags.forever = true`. Classic quests may also have been changed in Forever; QA should check them in game.
