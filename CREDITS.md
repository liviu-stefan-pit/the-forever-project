The in-app Credits page (`#/credits`) is generated from this table, so keep the column order and one source per row.

Licence rules for this repo:
- Free to use with credit: MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, CC0, CC BY.
- Use with care: GPL and CC BY-SA. Keep derived data in its own file and state its licence here.
- Not used as sources: paid guides (RestedXP, Zygor, Dugi, Joana).
- OpenXP is CC BY-NC-SA. Facts and quest ids only. No OpenXP sentences were copied. See `research/external-review/reports/similarity-audit.md`.
- Other free leveling guides and addons (listed in the last rows of the table with their upstream links and versions) were reference only: quest ids, order and coordinates were checked against them. Step text is original. Anything with no licence stays facts-only.
- Wowhead and similar sites are for checking facts by hand only. No scraping or bulk copying.
